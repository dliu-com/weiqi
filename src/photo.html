Rules

Chinese · komi 7.5

Always analysed under Chinese rules (area scoring) with komi 7.5: a photo cannot show earlier captures or ko history, and area scoring does not depend on them.

Your photo is sent to the cloud once to read the stones, and the position is sent once each time you analyse. Neither is stored. Your moves are saved only in this browser.
